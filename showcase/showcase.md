# Fathom

You are a small light in a very deep place. Swim the flooded corridors, graze
bright plankton, and find your way through a maze that only reveals itself when
your glow touches the rock. Eating opens your window onto the abyss, but the
brighter you burn, the farther away a hunter can see you.

Sonar spills around corners and lets you remember a route before you swim it.
Its cyan crest can find hidden predators, too. Listen for the answering pulse:
the eyeless Gloamfin follows sound through rock, and your own ping may bring it
to the very tile you hoped to leave behind.

An amber jelly drifts ahead. Is it a meal, or a Lanternjaw waiting to show its
teeth? Elsewhere, a Flarefish charges a sudden bloom that burns a whole pocket
of darkness away. Drop ink to break a light hunter's fix, round corners to slow
a sound hunter, and keep moving while the sea is still yours.

Eat the last mote to clear the maze and descend. Your score and spare lives
follow you into deeper water, where more hunters wait and sonar reaches less
far. A catch keeps the routes you remember and the plankton you've already
eaten. There is always one more corridor to try.
